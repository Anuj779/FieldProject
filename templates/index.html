A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Clear from
the start.

Is this legal advice?+

No. Clausewise explains what a document says. It may miss or misunderstand terms. Read the original agreement and ask a qualified professional when you need legal advice.

What happens to my document?+

Your PDF is processed temporarily and is not saved as history. Extracted text is sent to Google Gemini. On its free tier, Google may use this text to improve products. Use fictional or redacted agreements for demos. Your current results stay in this browser tab until you leave or sign out.

Can I upload a scanned agreement?+

For now, use a PDF with selectable text. Scanned, password-protected, or unreadable pages will receive a clear error.

Does the sample use live AI?+

No. The sample uses a fictional housing agreement and prepared explanations. Your own uploads use live AI after sign-in and service configuration.
